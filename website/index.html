How it works

  1. 1

    Copy as usual

    Clipaste records each text you copy, in any app.

  2. 2

    Open Clipaste

    Press CtrlShift9 (⌘⇧9 on a Mac) or click the tray icon.

  3. 3

    Pick and paste

    Click an item or press Enter. It goes back to your clipboard.

Questions

Where does Clipaste keep my history?

In a JSON file on your computer: ~/.config/Clipaste/history.json on Linux, ~/Library/Application Support/Clipaste/history.json on macOS and %APPDATA%\Clipaste\history.json on Windows. The snap keeps it inside ~/snap/clipaste/. Nothing goes to the internet.

The shortcut does not work on Ubuntu with Wayland. What can I do?

Wayland does not let apps register global shortcuts. Add your own shortcut in Settings → Keyboard → Custom Shortcuts with the command clipaste --toggle. You can also click the tray icon.

Why does an item have no source app?

Clipaste asks the system which app has the focus when the clipboard changes. On Linux this works for X11 apps. Native Wayland apps do not share this information, so their items have no source.

Can I start Clipaste when I log in?

Yes. Open the tray menu and select Start at Login.

I used Clipaste 1.x. Is my history still there?

Yes. Version 2.0 imports your old history automatically on the first start.
